## 1. Channel Concept

- **Working title:** Davids Cloud
- **One-line pitch:** Soul awakening with positivity — reflections and soulful
  inspiration that help you turn toward a positive headspace and intuitively reject
  what doesn't serve your spirit.
- **Niche / topic:** Soul awakening & positivity — spiritual enlightenment, inner
  discernment, and the beginning of the turn toward light.
- **Why you?** A personal, sincere voice that blends soul-level insight with warmth
  and encouragement — meeting people at the very start of their awakening, without
  judgment.

## 2. Target Audience

- **Who are they?** People at a turning point — sensing there's more, feeling the
  pull toward awakening but unsure where to begin. Open to spirituality, intuition,
  and positivity; often navigating a hard season and looking for light.
- **What do you give them?** A gentle on-ramp to a positive headspace, language for
  what their intuition already knows, and the encouragement to trust it — to embrace
  good and quietly turn away from what feels wrong.
- **Where do they already hang out?** Spiritual / awakening communities on YouTube
  (Caroline Myss, Mark Attwood audiences), meditation and affirmation channels,
  Instagram/TikTok spiritual and positivity spaces, relevant subreddits and Discords.

## 3. Content Pillars

| Pillar | Description | Example video |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| **Soul Awakening** | What awakening is and how it begins; intuition, the soul's voice, archetypes and inner truth (Caroline Myss's lineage). | "The First Signs Your Soul Is Waking Up" |

## 7. Growth & Promotion

- **SEO:** titles/descriptions around the words your audience searches — "soul
  awakening," "positive mindset," "spiritual awakening signs," "trusting intuition."
- **Thumbnails:** calm, bright, high-contrast text; consistent look builds trust.
- **Shorts funnel:** one uplifting affirmation or insight per short → subscribers.
- **Community:** reply to every early comment; use the Community tab for daily
  encouragement and to ask what people are struggling with (future video fuel).

## 8. Monetization (later)

- YouTube Partner Program requires **1,000 subscribers** and either **4,000 public
  watch hours in 12 months** or **10M Shorts views in 90 days**.
- Aligned paths for this niche: memberships/"inner circle," guided
  meditation/affirmation products, a Patreon, affirmation merch, and — only if it
  ever fits authentically — sponsorships from wellness brands you actually believe in.
