# Channel About Section

Copy for your YouTube channel's **About** page and profile. Paste from here into
YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info. Three lengths are provided — use the
one that fits each field.

## Tips
- YouTube shows the **first ~150 characters** in search and previews, so keep the
  strongest hook up front (the medium bio does this).
- Add your **links** (website, socials) separately in Studio → Customization →
  Basic info → Links; they render on your banner.
- Sprinkle natural keywords people search — *soul awakening, positivity, spiritual
  awakening, intuition, positive mindset* — but keep it human, never stuffed.
