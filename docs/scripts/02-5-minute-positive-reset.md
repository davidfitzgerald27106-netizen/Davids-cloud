# Video Script — "5-Minute Morning Reset Into a Positive Headspace"

- **Pillar:** Positive Headspace
- **Goal:** Give viewers a simple, repeatable morning practice they'll come back to.
- **Target length:** 5–6 minutes · **Tone:** calm, warm, guiding.

> *Italics* = delivery/visual notes. Plain text = what you say.

---

## [COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:20]
*Soft light, gentle music.*

The first five minutes of your morning set the tone for everything after. So let's
give those five minutes to your soul — together, right now.

## [INTRO — 0:20–0:50]
Welcome back to Davids Cloud. This is a reset you can do every single morning — no
app, no cost, just you and your breath. Do it with me today, then keep it forever.

## [STEP 1 — Breathe — 0:50–1:50]
*Slow down. Model the breathing.*

Sit comfortably. Close your eyes if you'd like. Let's take three slow breaths.
In through the nose… and out. *(pause)* Again — in… and out. *(pause)* One more —
in… and out. Feel your shoulders drop. You've already begun.

## [STEP 2 — Gratitude — 1:50–3:00]
Now, one thing. Just one. Something you're grateful for this morning — however
small. The warmth of the bed. A person. A second chance at today. Hold it in your
mind and let yourself actually *feel* it. Gratitude is the doorway a positive mind
walks through.

## [STEP 3 — Set the intention — 3:00–4:00]
Now choose one word for your day. *Peace. Courage. Kindness. Light.* Whatever your
spirit needs. Say it to yourself: "Today, I choose ___." That word is now your
compass. When the day gets loud, you can come back to it.

## [STEP 4 — Release — 4:00–4:45]
One last breath. As you exhale, let go of one worry you woke up carrying. You don't
have to solve it right now. Just set it down. It'll still be there if you need it —
but you don't have to hold it every second.

## [OUTRO — 4:45–5:30]
That's it. Five minutes. You just chose your headspace instead of letting the day
choose it for you.

*Warm.* Do this tomorrow, and the day after. Subscribe so we can reset together each
week. And tell me below — what word did you choose today? Be gentle with your soul.

*Music fades.*

---

## Production notes
- **Visuals:** sunrise, calm breath, a single candle; keep motion slow.
- **Music:** soft, uplifting, licensed/royalty-free.
- **Shorts cut:** the 3-breath section (Step 1) makes a perfect standalone Short.
