# Video Script — "Trusting the Gut Feeling That Says 'No'"

- **Pillar:** Intuitive Discernment
- **Goal:** Help viewers recognize their intuition and gently reject what harms their
  spirit — without fear.
- **Target length:** 6–7 minutes · **Tone:** grounded, reassuring, steady.

> *Italics* = delivery/visual notes. Plain text = what you say.

---

## [COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:25]
You know the feeling. Something looks fine on the surface — but something inside you
says *no.* A quiet, certain no. Most of us are taught to override it. Today, let's
learn to trust it.

## [INTRO — 0:25–1:00]
Welcome to Davids Cloud. Part of soul awakening is discernment — learning to feel the
difference between what feeds your spirit and what quietly poisons it. Your intuition
already knows. This video is about learning to listen.

## [SECTION 1 — Your intuition is data — 1:00–2:30]
*Sincere, confident.*

That gut feeling isn't paranoia. It's information. Your soul notices things your
mind talks itself out of — a tension in the room, a heaviness around certain people,
a "something's off" you can't quite explain.

Teachers like Caroline Myss remind us the body and spirit register truth before the
mind catches up. That chill, that unease — that's your inner guidance, doing its job.

## [SECTION 2 — Why we override it — 2:30–3:45]
We ignore it for good-sounding reasons. We don't want to seem rude. We give the
benefit of the doubt. We tell ourselves we're imagining it. And sometimes we are —
so let's be honest and discerning, not fearful. But notice the pattern: how often,
looking back, did that quiet "no" turn out to be right?

## [SECTION 3 — How to honor it — 3:45–5:15]
*Practical, calm.*

Here's the practice. When the "no" arrives, don't argue with it and don't panic.
Just three steps:

One — **pause.** Don't act from the feeling instantly; feel it fully first.
Two — **name it.** "I feel heaviness here." Naming turns noise into signal.
Three — **choose.** Set a gentle boundary. Step back. Say no. You don't owe anyone
an explanation for protecting your peace.

Rejecting what's wrong doesn't require a battle. Usually it's just a quiet turning
away — toward the light instead.

## [SECTION 4 — Without fear — 5:15–6:00]
And do it *without* fear. Fear and intuition can feel similar, but they're different.
Fear grabs and spirals. Intuition is calm and clear. When you're rooted in
positivity, you can say no to darkness without letting it frighten you — because you
love the light more than you fear the dark.

## [OUTRO — 6:00–6:45]
So this week, when that quiet no shows up — pause, name it, honor it. Your soul has
been trying to protect you all along.

Subscribe to keep walking this path with me. And tell me below — has your intuition
ever been right when your mind said otherwise? Protect your peace. It's sacred.

*Music fades.*

---

## Production notes
- **Visuals:** calm, grounded — steady shots, natural light, open hands, a boundary
  metaphor (a door gently closing, a step back).
- **Careful framing:** keep it empowering, not fear-based. This topic can tip into
  paranoia if the tone gets heavy — stay warm and steady.
- **Shorts cut:** the three-step "pause / name / choose" makes a strong Short.
